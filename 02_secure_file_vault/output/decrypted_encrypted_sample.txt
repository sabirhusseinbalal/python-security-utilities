In a quiet evening,
a student opened his file.

Inside were simple words,
nothing secret, nothing special.

But today was different.

He wanted to protect it,
to hide it from curious eyes,
to learn how real systems keep data safe.

With one command,
the text turned into unreadable symbols,
strange characters no one could understand.

It looked broken,
but it was not.

It was protected.

Later, using the same key,
he brought the words back to life.

Clear again.
Readable again.

And in that moment,
he understood something small but powerful:

Security is not magic,
it is careful thinking.

And today,
he took one step forward.
