The Night Before the Big Project

The room was quiet except for the soft sound of keyboard clicks.
A tired programmer stared at the glowing screen,
half-awake,
half-confused,
but still trying.

Outside, the rain tapped gently on the window.
Inside, variables argued with functions,
loops repeated themselves endlessly,
and one mysterious bug refused to disappear.

He sighed,
drank cold coffee,
and read the error message for the tenth time.

“SyntaxError.”

Again.

For a moment,
he thought about giving up.
Maybe tomorrow would be easier.
Maybe coding was too difficult.

But then he remembered something important:

Every skilled programmer
was once a beginner
staring at confusing code at 2 AM.

So he fixed one small line.
Then another.
Then another.

Slowly,
the red errors disappeared.

The terminal became silent.

And finally…

Program finished successfully.

He smiled proudly at the screen,
saved the file,
and whispered:

“Tomorrow… we learn recursion.”
